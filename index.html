Active Inference Knowledge Environment

A comprehensive integrated platform for Active Inference & Free Energy Principle education, research, visualization, and application development.

📚 Start Learning 🔬 Research Tools 👁️ Visualizations 🛠️ Applications

Overview

This repository provides a structured, accessible knowledge environment that bridges theoretical foundations with practical applications of Active Inference and the Free Energy Principle. Built with generative AI assistance, it offers modular, well-documented components for learning, research, and development.

Foundations Track

Start your journey with the theoretical foundations:

  1. Information Theory Basics - Entropy, mutual information, KL divergence

  2. Bayesian Inference - Probabilistic reasoning and belief updating

  3. Free Energy Principle - Theoretical foundations and derivations

  4. Active Inference - Decision making and behavior
