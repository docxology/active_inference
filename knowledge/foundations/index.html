Foundations

Core theoretical foundations of Active Inference and the Free Energy Principle. This section covers the fundamental concepts, principles, and theoretical frameworks that underlie the active inference paradigm.

Essential Concepts

Information Theory

Understanding the information-theoretic foundations:

  • Entropy and information content

  • Mutual Information and dependencies

  • KL Divergence and model comparison

  • Information Bottleneck method

Bayesian Inference

Probabilistic reasoning and belief updating:

  • Bayesian updating and evidence accumulation

  • Prior beliefs and knowledge representation

  • Likelihood functions and model fitting

  • Posterior distributions and uncertainty

Free Energy Principle

The unifying principle behind intelligent behavior:

  • Variational Free Energy as a measure of surprise

  • Markov blankets and system boundaries

  • Generative models and prediction

  • Active inference as energy minimization

Active Inference

Decision making and behavior through inference:

  • Policy selection and action

  • Expected Free Energy minimization

  • Exploration vs Exploitation trade-offs

  • Learning and adaptation mechanisms

Learning Objectives

After completing this section, you should be able to:

  1. Explain the core principles of Active Inference

  2. Understand the role of the Free Energy Principle

  3. Apply Bayesian inference in simple scenarios

  4. Interpret information-theoretic measures

  5. Connect theoretical concepts to practical applications

Prerequisites

Before diving into the foundations:

  • Basic probability and statistics

  • Linear algebra fundamentals

  • Python programming (for implementations)

  • Scientific computing concepts
